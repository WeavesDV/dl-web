Frequently asked questions

Are the experiences in person or online?

We design and facilitate training programs, mentoring, workshops, gatherings, events, retreats and immersive experiences, both in person and online.

Is mentoring individual or in groups?

Individual or group spaces designed to bring clarity, perspective and tools to move forward in personal and professional processes.

Is this psychotherapy?

Our programs, workshops, mentoring, retreats and experiences are educational and oriented towards personal growth. They do not constitute psychotherapy, diagnosis, medical treatment or mental health care, and they do not replace the assistance of qualified health or mental health professionals when it is needed.

Do I have to share health information in the form?

Through the site's general forms we do not request sensitive health information, medical diagnoses or other special categories of personal data.

Who are these services for?

Our services are intended exclusively for people over 18 years of age.
