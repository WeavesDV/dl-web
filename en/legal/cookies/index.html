Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Divine Light LLC (hereinafter, “the Company”, “we” or “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on the website divinelightjourney.com.

This Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which provides additional information on how we collect, use and protect personal information.

1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small files or pieces of information that may be stored on the user's device when they visit a website.

Among other functions, cookies make a website work, remember certain preferences, provide information about how the site is used and, where appropriate, deliver content or advertising tailored to particular interests.

In addition to cookies, our website may use similar technologies such as pixels, tags, identifiers, local storage or other tools that make it possible to recognize a device or collect certain information about interaction with the site.

For the purposes of this Policy, we use the term “cookies” to refer collectively to these technologies where appropriate.

2. WHAT TYPES OF COOKIES MAY WE USE?

Depending on the tools installed on our website, we may use the following categories of cookies:

Strictly necessary cookies

These are required for the basic, secure operation of the website.

They may be used, for example, to:

  • enable browsing;
  • maintain certain technical features;
  • manage sessions;
  • keep the site secure;
  • remember the user's privacy or consent preferences;
  • enable certain functions required to provide a service requested by the user.

These cookies generally do not require consent where they are strictly necessary to provide the service requested by the user, subject to applicable law.

Preference or functionality cookies

They make it possible to remember certain choices made by the user in order to offer a more personalized experience.

For example, they may remember:

  • selected language;
  • region;
  • certain display preferences;
  • previously selected settings.

Where applicable law requires it, these cookies will be used only after the relevant consent has been obtained.

Analytics or measurement cookies

They allow us to understand how visitors use our website.

They may collect information on aspects such as:

  • pages visited;
  • approximate length of visits;
  • navigation within the site;
  • source of visits;
  • devices and browsers used;
  • interaction with certain content.

This information can help us understand how the site works and improve our content and services.

Where applicable law requires it, these cookies will be activated only after the user's consent has been obtained.

Advertising and marketing cookies

These cookies may be used to measure advertising campaigns, understand interaction with our ads or show advertising that is more relevant to certain users.

They may also allow certain third-party providers to recognize a browser or device across different sites or services.

Where applicable law requires it, these cookies and technologies will be used only after the user has given consent.

3. FIRST-PARTY AND THIRD-PARTY COOKIES

The cookies used on our site may be:

First-party cookies: managed directly by our website or by services used under our control.

Third-party cookies: set or managed by external providers whose services are integrated into our website.

These third parties may include, depending on the tools used by the Company:

  • web analytics providers;
  • advertising platforms;
  • social media;
  • video platforms;
  • booking systems;
  • payment providers;
  • email marketing tools;
  • customer service services;
  • technology platforms used to provide our services.

Each external provider may process information in accordance with its own privacy and cookie policies.

5. CONSENT AND COOKIE MANAGEMENT

Where applicable law requires consent to use certain cookies or similar technologies, they will not be activated until the user has made the relevant choice.

On first visiting the website, the user may find a cookie banner or management tool that allows them, as applicable, to:

  • accept all cookies;
  • reject non-essential cookies;
  • set their preferences by category.

Strictly necessary cookies may remain active where they are indispensable for the operation of the website or to provide a service expressly requested by the user.

The user may later change or withdraw their consent using the [“COOKIE SETTINGS” LINK/BUTTON] available on the website.

Withdrawing consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before that withdrawal.

6. USERS IN THE EUROPEAN ECONOMIC AREA AND OTHER JURISDICTIONS

For users located in the European Economic Area or other jurisdictions that require prior consent, the Company will request that consent before storing or accessing information through non-essential cookies where applicable law so requires.

The user must be able to reject non-essential cookies and manage their preferences through the tools provided on the website.

The availability and operation of certain technologies may vary depending on the user's location and the applicable legal requirements.

7. BROWSER SETTINGS

In addition to using our cookie settings tool, users can configure their browsers to block, limit or delete cookies.

How to do this will depend on the browser and device used.

Blocking certain cookies, especially necessary or functional ones, may affect the operation of some parts of the website.

Settings made directly in the browser are independent of the preferences that can be managed through our site's cookie panel.

8. THIRD-PARTY TECHNOLOGIES

Some pages may include content or features provided by third parties, such as videos, maps, social media buttons, forms, calendars, booking tools or other embedded content.

These providers may use cookies or other technologies when the user interacts with their services.

Where applicable law requires it, we will endeavor to ensure that non-essential technologies that may be installed through third-party content are subject to the relevant consent options.

Users can consult the privacy and cookie policies of each external provider for additional information about their practices.

Persistent cookies remain for a set period or until the user deletes them.

Specific retention periods will depend on each cookie and provider and should be reflected, where applicable, in the information provided by the cookie management tool or in the cookie table in this Policy.
