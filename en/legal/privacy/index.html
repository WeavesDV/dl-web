Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

At Divine Light LLC, with its registered office in Albuquerque, New Mexico, United States (hereinafter, “the Company”, “we” or “us”), we respect the privacy of the people who visit our website divinelightjourney.com, contact us or engage our services.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we may collect, how we use it, with whom we may share it, how long we may keep it and what rights users may have depending on where they live.

Our services are intended exclusively for people over 18 years of age.

1. DATA CONTROLLER

Company: Divine Light LLC Entity type: Limited Liability Company (LLC) Location: Albuquerque, New Mexico, United States Website: divinelightjourney.com Contact email: info@divinelightjourney.com

For any question related to this Privacy Policy or to the processing of personal information, you can contact us at the email address indicated above.

2. PERSONAL INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

Depending on how the user interacts with our website and our services, we may collect different categories of information.

Information provided directly by the user

We may collect information when a person:

  • completes a contact form;
  • requests information about our programs or services;
  • registers for a program, workshop, event, retreat or experience;
  • engages any of our services;
  • subscribes to our communications;
  • contacts us by email or other available channels;
  • takes part in activities, programs or experiences organized by the Company.

Depending on the case, this information may include:

  • first and last name;
  • email address;
  • phone number;
  • country or place of residence;
  • information related to the service requested or engaged;
  • information needed to manage registrations, bookings or payments;
  • any other information the user chooses to provide us voluntarily.

Through the site's general forms we do not request sensitive health information, medical diagnoses or other special categories of personal data.

Information collected automatically

When a person uses our website, certain technologies may automatically collect technical and browsing information, such as:

  • IP address;
  • device type;
  • browser used;
  • operating system;
  • pages visited;
  • date and time of access;
  • approximate browsing time;
  • source from which the website was accessed;
  • interactions with certain pages or content;
  • identifiers associated with cookies or other similar technologies.

The information actually collected will depend on the technologies and tools installed on the website.

3. WHAT WE USE THE INFORMATION FOR

We may use the personal information collected to:

  • respond to inquiries and requests for information;
  • manage our relationship with clients and users;
  • process registrations, bookings or engagements;
  • provide our services;
  • organize programs, mentoring, workshops, gatherings, events, retreats and other experiences;
  • manage payments and invoicing;
  • send communications related to services engaged;
  • send marketing communications where consent or another applicable legal basis exists;
  • manage and improve our website;
  • analyze how the site works and is used;
  • keep the website secure and prevent fraudulent or improper use;
  • comply with our legal, tax, accounting and administrative obligations;
  • establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We will not use personal information for purposes incompatible with those for which it was collected, unless there is a legal basis that allows it.

4. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING INFORMATION

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing personal information, we may rely, depending on the circumstances, on:

Consent: where the user has expressly authorized a specific processing operation.

Performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps: where processing is necessary to manage a request, registration, booking, purchase or provision of services.

Compliance with legal obligations: where we need to keep or process certain information to comply with legal, tax, accounting or regulatory obligations.

Legitimate interests: where necessary to operate, protect and improve our business and our services, provided those interests do not override the rights and freedoms of the person concerned.

Where processing is based on consent, the user may withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of the processing carried out before that withdrawal.

We may send information about programs, activities, events, workshops, retreats, content or services where the user has given consent or where another legal basis allows it.

The user may stop receiving marketing communications at any time through the unsubscribe link included in emails or by writing to us at info@divinelightjourney.com.

Unsubscribing from marketing communications will not prevent us from sending necessary messages related to a service engaged, a transaction or an existing relationship with the user.
